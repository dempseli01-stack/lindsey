# Part 2 clarifications — Lindsey Dempsey (challenge)

## The ideal AI-era SDR
Role has changed because AI handles rote work → SDRs can be as efficient as possible and focus on what humans are good at.

**Core belief:** Humans want to be sold to by other humans. Even with AI, messaging and cold calls need humanization. Stand out by helping prospects and starting a discussion so they can reap Cursor’s benefits.

**Exceptional looks like:**
- Uses AI for rote work
- Stands out through genuine discovery, care, understanding current state / pains, how Cursor helps
- Knows relevant customer stories based on use cases
- Proficient in:
  - Organization and time management
  - Research and business intuition
  - Written and verbal communication
  - Active listening and adaptability — carry a conversation and react with value
  - Objection handling — comprehend and overcome
  - Storytelling — paint a picture of technical pain and business value

## Hiring great talent
**Bar / vibe:** Excited every single day. Can I see them moving into the next role?

**Experience bar:** 1–2 years in sales or something technical / requiring intelligence. Some of the best hires have been teachers or people with work experience wanting to move into sales — they understand concepts quickly.

**Must-have traits (every candidate):**
1. **Intelligence** — can gain knowledge
2. **Drive** — so they can gain the skills
3. **Coachable** — adapt to change
4. **Integrity** — honest
5. **Genuine passion for AI and Cursor** — not just “fast-growing company”; obsessed; nerd out on which model is best and what’s changing in the space

**Process:**
- LinkedIn Recruiter
- Prefer native language for the role (non-native speakers struggling with technical second-language selling has been a past issue)

## Coaching and development framework
Same framework for coaching/development:

1. **Tell** — what, who, why  
2. **Show** — what good looks like  
3. **Observe** — let them struggle  
4. **Provide feedback** — first ask what they did well and what they’d do differently  
5. **Repeat** until mastered  

### Ramping new reps
- Lots of time together: constant training, homework, fix gaps quickly
- Heavy on the **why** behind everything
- **90-day ramp plan broken down by week** — they know exactly what to do when the week starts
- **Buddy system** — someone on the team for questions and advice

### Level up best people
- Understand early: what’s their motivation? what’s their goal?
- Catch them doing something good
- Let them be a mentor on the team
- Identify gaps in their workflow → constant coaching

### Underperformance
- As soon as someone is off track → coaching plan before it becomes a bigger issue
- Coaching plans provide structure

### Culture of getting better every week
- Scoreboards
- Competitions
- Collaboration
- End every week with a shoutout for someone else on the team so collaboration doesn’t go unnoticed
